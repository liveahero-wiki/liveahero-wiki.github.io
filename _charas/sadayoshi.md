## Notes:

- His full name is Sadayoshi Ikusabata (軍畑 サダヨシ)
    1. Ikusabata (軍畑)
        * **Location:** Ikusabata is a station on the [**Ome Line** (**青梅線**)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%8Cme_Line) located in 1-chome, Sawai, Ome City, Tokyo (**東京都青梅市沢井1丁目**).
        * **Origin of the Place Name:** The area along the Tama River (**多摩川**) where the current Ikusabata Bridge (**軍畑大橋**) spans was the historical battlefield of the [**Battle of Karakai** (**辛垣の戦い**)](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%BE%9B%E5%9E%A3%E3%81%AE%E6%88%A6%E3%81%84), where the fortress was conquered and captured by the army of Hojo Ujiteru ([**北条氏照**]((https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C5%8Dj%C5%8D_Ujiteru))).
        * **Tachibana Clan Association:** The site where the residence of the Tachibana clan (**立花家**), lords of the Yanagawa Domain (**柳川藩**), was built was referred to as *"Ohanabatake"* (**御花畠 / 畑**).
    2. Sadayoshi (貞義)
        * **Name Origin:** The name is likely derived from [**Tachibana Sadayoshi** (**立花貞俶**)](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%AB%8B%E8%8A%B1%E8%B2%9E%E4%BF%B6), a *hatamoto* and *daimyo* of the mid-Edo period. He became the adopted heir of his uncle, **Tachibana Sadakira** (**立花貞晟**), a *hatamoto yoriai* (**旗本寄合**), and inherited the family headship after his adoptive father passed away.
        * **Story Parallel:** Within the plot, Sadayoshi is similarly welcomed into the Ikusabata family as an adopted son, which mirrors this historical background.
        * **Career Note:** A [**Hatamoto** (**旗本**)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hatamoto) historically refers to an elite samurai warrior group that protected the supreme commander—which likely parallels or references Sadayoshi's career transition as a military officer under the Japan Self-Defense Authorities.
