## Notes

- His Hero name "Meister Farbe" can be translated to "Master of Color".
- His Japanese name "ファーデン" can be translated to the German Word "Faden" meaning "string" or "thread", it is also used in a common idiom : "Der rote Faden" meaning literally "The red thread".
  - Using the translation as Fayden would've been a nice way to keep the original meaning while keeping "Fay"(fairy) for his relation to storybooks and tales
- His skill "Es war einmal" is the German equivalent of "once upon a time.
- During his Skill "From Paradise" Farden says "Aus Elysium" which translates to "From Paradise" in German, as translated correctly. It's also very known for being present in the popular German poet, Friedrich Schiller's poem "Ode of Joy" as "Tochter Aus Elysium" (Daughter from Paradise).
