## Notes
- Maculata's name is Latin for "spotted" which also alludes to her being a leopard.
- Her skill name is from french words:
  - Fil Dernier : Last Thread
  - Bon Taille : Correct Size
  - Tailleur A Griffes : Tailor's Claws
