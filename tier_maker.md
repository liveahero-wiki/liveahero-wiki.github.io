Rank every hero and sidekick into your own tier list. Drag characters into the tiers (or tap a character, then a tier), rename and recolor the tiers, then export the tier list as an image. The exported PNG carries the tier list inside it, so you can import it here later and keep editing. Share the original file: sites that re-encode images (chat apps, image hosts) strip that data.
