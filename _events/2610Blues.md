## Event Preview

“Come experience the technology of the future together at our booth!”

The Grand Galactic Expo, the grand stage for the unveiling of new technologies.

At the request of an up-and-coming hero who designs cities, you agree to accompany him as his operator after he accepts an ambassador role for a certain general contracting company.

You end up forming an impromptu team of four with the company's other promotional figurehead, a brave and fearless mining engineer hero, and a young, low-energy event organizer. However...

...Product demonstrations, customer service, advertising and promotion.

In the midst of being overwhelmed by unfamiliar work, things take an even more dramatic turn when a rival company specializing in Kaibutsu extermination makes its appearance...

The PR battle between the companies is heating up in an unpredictable frenzy!?

Which company will be awarded the prestigious title of “Tech of the Year,” the honor bestowed upon the winner of the popularity vote...?!

—Whether they're heroes or corporate employees,

those who give their all at work shine brightly!

【Notice】

This event's story can be enjoyed even more if you have cleared the previous events “Light Years Illumination” and “Whiteout Revelation.”

(You can still participate in this event even if you have not cleared the scenarios for the events listed above.)
