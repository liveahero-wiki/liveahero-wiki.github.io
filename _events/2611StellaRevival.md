## Event Preview

Orient City's summer tradition, the Hero City Summer Festa.
Even after the passionate summer festival comes to an end, the Heroes' festivities are far from over!

A new event is set to be held at the Archimedes Colony on the Moon—a grand festival event that gathers up festivals from across all the planets of the galaxy and holds them in one place.
Its name is the "Galaxy Carnival Expo"!

While working hard running a food stall at the Summer Festa, a request from some Heroes suddenly comes your way.
It comes from a gunman police officer—the self-proclaimed #1 festival lover in Orient City—and
a languid-looking magician who knows nothing about Japanese festivals.

Accepting their request to help out at the event on the Moon, you
also join forces with an energetic young tour conductor and
launch a streaming campaign with the Heroes to promote Japanese festivals.

From a fashion-city festival to a flower-city festival,
and even creative festivals born from the idea of "I'd love to try holding a festival like this!"—
now that the Moon has turned into a red-hot festival hotspot,
will the Heroes be able to showcase Japanese festivals to the entire galaxy?!

Beneath a sky decorated by fireworks and Earth's glow over the Moon,
a festival connecting people, planets, and memories begins here—
