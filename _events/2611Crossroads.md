## Event Preview

"May this melody reach you, whom I cannot meet for now."

A special music festival held during the seven days of the polar night — the Seven Nights Festival.

Invited to the Seven Nights Festival by a traveling singer-songwriter and a magician from another world, you visit Crossroad Valley, a wilderness where white flowers bloom in abundance.

A conductor harboring feelings for a friend, a chef who has lost their benefactor, and a hunter whose homeland was destroyed—

The people you encounter there all share one thing in common: they each hold someone dear in their hearts, someone they can no longer meet for now.

As you interact with these various individuals and come to understand their feelings, you are entrusted with the song that will bring the grand finale to a close: "A Song for the Dawn."

—You are faced with a choice.

Will you play a ballad for tomorrow, or an elegy for the memories of yesterday?
