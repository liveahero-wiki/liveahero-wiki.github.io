## Event Preview

In this campaign you can play link quest and the wolfman campaign where you can collect items through free quest and exchange them at the campaign store for the new mob hero/sidekick Wolfman for each element.

## What Are Link Quests?
As part of the Autumn Festival 2026, the limited-time story content "Link Quests" will sequentially introduce special episodes highlighting interactions between specific characters.

Link Quests are quests that allow players to gain deeper insight into the relationships among multiple characters.

Through requests on the Link Board, you can enjoy scenarios where you can feel the "bonds" of characters engaging and communicating with each other!

Link Quests can be played by anyone, and you can proceed to the "Link Quests" by tapping "Event" on the Home screen.

# Regarding Past Link Quests
Past Link Quests have been permanently added to "Special Quests" and are available to view.
* To play permanently available Link Quests, you must have completed the Link Quest "Prologue."

When playing permanently available Link Quests other than the Link Quest "Prologue" for the first time, you must consume 1 {% include item.html id=233 %} to unlock the quest. Once unlocked by consuming a {% include item.html id=233 %}, the Link Quest can be played any number of times thereafter without consuming additional Memory Archives.

## About Stamina Drink Mini
At this campaign's "Campaign Item Exchange Shop," "Stamina Drink Mini" can be exchanged.
"Stamina Drink Mini" is an item that can restore 20 Stamina when you do not have enough Stamina while playing quests.

The "Stamina Drink Mini" in this campaign can only be exchanged once a day, and the stock will be replenished every day.
Any unexchanged amounts for the day cannot be exchanged together on the following days, so please be careful not to forget to exchange them. Note that Stamina Drink Mini available only until the end of this campaign.

## About Rank 1/2 Characters

The Wolfman character that can be obtained in this campaign comes as a rank 1 hero unit and a rank 1 sidekick unit.

Rank 1/2 units can participate in battle and sale just like normal characters but there are several differences when it comes to upgrading them.

### Hero's Promotion

Hero units with initial rank of 1 or 2, aside from regular rank up materials, require {% include item.html id=53 %} to promote. The necessary amount increases every rank.

Additionally their 3rd skill will at first be locked and unusable in battle until they are promoted to rank 3.

### Hero's Fan Count

Hero units with initial rank of 1 or 2 can only gain fan count from their own personal Hero's Memories items. Said items will also can't be exchanged for {% include item.html id=40 %}.

Their fan count will only increase by 1 with each item used.

### Sidekick's Limit Breakthrough

Sidekick units of rank 1 or 2 can only be upgraded with their own personal Sidekick's Heart items. Said items will also can't be exchanged for {% include item.html id=40 %}.
